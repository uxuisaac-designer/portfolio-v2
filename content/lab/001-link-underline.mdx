Every link on this site carries two lines. The first rests under the words
at all times, light enough to mark the link without competing with the text.
The second is the text colour, and on hover it wipes in from the left along
the first.

Most underlines change colour on hover. I wanted this one to move, so the
dark line is a pseudo-element scaled from zero to full width rather than a
recoloured text-decoration. On the way out it keeps going and leaves by the
right edge instead of retracting. The trick is transform-origin: it is left
out of the transition, so it flips instantly, and it only flips while the
line sits at full width or none — where a left and a right origin draw the
same box.

The resting line is where it nearly failed. Set at the muted text colour,
the two lines sat 2.3:1 apart and the wipe vanished at speed. Lightening it
to oklch(0.750) made the movement legible, and put the line at 2.1:1 against
the page — under the 3:1 floor for non-text contrast. I kept it, and logged
the shortfall as something to fix with a second affordance rather than a
darker line.

The curve does more of the work than the duration. On cubic-bezier(0.32,
0.72, 0, 1), 90% of the travel lands in the first 146ms of the 400.
