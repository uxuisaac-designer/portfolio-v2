The theme toggle shows the theme you are in: a sun in light, a moon in dark.
Both glyphs are always rendered, stacked in one grid cell, and the theme
class on the page picks which one is up. The choice is made in CSS rather
than in JavaScript, so the right icon is there from the first paint.

The swap is a dial. The outgoing glyph turns a quarter clockwise, shrinks to
half and fades, while the incoming one turns in from a quarter behind. It
takes 300ms on the entrance curve. Going back, it turns the other way.

It is a transition, not keyframes. A double-click reverses from wherever the
swap has got to instead of jumping to the end and starting again. That
depends on the theme library’s disableTransitionOnChange staying off —
turned on, it would kill the dial along with every other transition on the
page.

Under reduced motion the turn and the shrink come off, and only the
cross-fade is left.
